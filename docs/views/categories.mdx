# Categories

Categories let you label and organize your transactions by type of spending or income.

## Creating a Category

Each category has a name and an optional color. You can pick from a built-in palette of 13 Material Design colors or enter a custom hex value. The color appears as an indicator dot next to transactions in the History and Budget views.

## Usage

Categories are assigned to schedules and postings. They're used in the Budget view to group projected spending by category, making it easy to see where your money goes each month.

## Deleting

Removing a category does not affect existing postings or schedules that reference it — they'll simply show without a category label.
