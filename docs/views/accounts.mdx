# Accounts

Accounts are the bank accounts and cash you budget with. Find them in **Plan → Accounts**, or tap the balance on Home. Tap **Add** to create one, or tap an account to edit it. On wide screens the editor opens beside the list.

## Balances

Balances are entered manually and are not changed by recorded transactions. Update the balance whenever you reconcile with your bank. The Home screen shows the total across all accounts.

## Deleting

Deleting an account also deletes every transaction recorded to it. Schedules that used it keep existing but need a new account. You'll be asked to confirm, and this can't be undone.
