# Budget

The Budget view shows a monthly projection of your finances, broken down by category.

## Monthly Overview

Navigate between months using the arrow buttons or jump to a specific month with the date picker. The view displays a net change summary showing whether you're projected to have a surplus or deficit for the month.

## Category Breakdown

Each category is shown as an expandable section listing the schedules that contribute to it. For each schedule, you can see:

- Number of occurrences in the month
- Per-occurrence amount
- Total amount for the month

This makes it easy to identify where your money is going and spot categories that are over or under budget.

## How It's Computed

The budget is calculated by the `ComputeBudgetUseCase`, which projects all schedule occurrences for the selected month and groups them by category. Income schedules contribute positive amounts, expense schedules contribute negative amounts, and transfers are shown separately.
