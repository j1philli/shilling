# Activity

Activity lists recorded transactions: occurrences you marked in Plan, one-off transactions, and CSV imports.

## Browsing

Transactions are grouped by day, newest first. A transfer's two legs appear as a single `From → To` row. Search matches the description, account, or category. Use **1M / 3M / 6M / 1Y** to set how far back to look. Transactions recorded ahead of their date also appear.

## Adding and Editing

Tap **Import** to bring in a bank CSV export (see [Import](./import)).

Tap **Add** to record a one-off expense, income, or transfer. Tap any transaction to edit it, attach receipts, or delete it (with Undo). On wide screens the transaction opens beside the list.

Transactions recorded from a schedule keep the schedule's category unless you pick a different one.
