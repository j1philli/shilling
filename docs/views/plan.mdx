# Plan

Plan has four sections:

- **Overview**: what's scheduled for a week or a month, and what's already been recorded (below).
- **Schedules**: the bills, paychecks and transfers that feed the overview. See [Schedules](./schedules).
- **Categories**: the labels used in the By category view. See [Categories](./categories).
- **Accounts**: the bank accounts and cash you budget with. See [Accounts](./accounts).

Home's tiles open the matching section: **This week** and **This month** open Overview for that period, and the balance opens Accounts.

## Overview: period

Use **Week / Month** to pick the period. The arrows move one period at a time, tapping the date jumps to any date, and **This week** / **This month** returns to today. A week starts on your chosen week-start day (Friday by default, changeable in **Settings → Appearance**) and begins on the next occurrence of that day.

The summary card shows the period's scheduled **Income**, **Expenses**, and the resulting **Surplus** or **Deficit**. Transfers move money between your own accounts, so they don't affect the total.

## Overview: by day

Occurrences are grouped under day headers. Each row shows the account (or `From → To` for transfers), category, and amount.

- **✓ (Mark paid / received / moved)** records the occurrence as a transaction.
- **⋮ → Change amount…** changes the amount for this date only. The schedule is unchanged.
- **⋮ → Skip this time** hides this occurrence.

Every action shows a confirmation with **Undo**. Recorded items show a check mark. Tap one to open the transaction and attach receipts, or use **⋮ → Mark not paid** to undo the recording.

## Overview: by category

Each category shows its net amount for the period. Expand it to see the schedules behind it, including how often each occurs (for example `4 × $180.00`) and how many are recorded. Tap a schedule to edit it.
