# Receipts

Receipts stores receipt images and documents. Each receipt can be attached to a transaction.

## Adding

Tap **Add**, then take a photo, choose one from your library (mobile), or choose any file. Optionally add the receipt date, amount, and notes, and attach it to a transaction right away.

## Viewing and Attaching

Filter by **All / Not attached / Attached**. Tap a receipt to open it: images preview in the app, and other files (PDF, HEIC) open in your device's viewer.

**Attach to transaction** searches the last six months of transactions. Transactions matching the receipt's amount and date are listed first. You can detach a receipt at any time without deleting it.

You can also attach receipts from a transaction's detail screen in Activity or Plan.
