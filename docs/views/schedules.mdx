# Schedules

Schedules are the bills, paychecks, and transfers you expect. Find them in **Plan → Schedules**; they drive Plan's Overview.

## Types

- **Expense**: money going out (rent, groceries, subscriptions)
- **Income**: money coming in (paychecks, freelance payments)
- **Transfer**: money moving between your accounts (savings, card payments)

Filter the list with **All / Expenses / Income / Transfers**. Tap **Add** to create a schedule, or tap one to edit it.

## Repeats

- **Does not repeat**: once, on the chosen date
- **Daily**: every *N* days
- **Weekly**: every *N* weeks on the selected days
- **Every 2 weeks**: on the start date's weekday
- **Monthly on a date**: every *N* months on a day of the month, or the last day. Months without that day (for example the 31st) are skipped.
- **Monthly on a weekday**: every *N* months on, for example, the second Tuesday
- **Twice a month**: the 15th and the last day
- **Yearly**: every *N* years on the start date

The editor shows the next date the schedule will occur, so you can check the pattern before saving. An optional **Ends on** date stops the schedule.

## Per-date changes

To skip one occurrence or change its amount, use the **⋮** menu in Plan → Overview (By day). The schedule itself isn't changed.

## Deleting

Deleting a schedule also deletes transactions already recorded from it. You'll be asked to confirm.
