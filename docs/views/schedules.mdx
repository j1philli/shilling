# Schedules

Schedules define your recurring and one-time transactions. They're the core building block of Shilling's budgeting system.

## Schedule Types

- **Expense** — money going out (rent, groceries, subscriptions)
- **Income** — money coming in (paychecks, freelance payments)
- **Transfer** — money moving between your accounts (savings contributions, credit card payments)

The view is organized with tabs to filter by type.

## Creating a Schedule

The schedule form includes:

- **Title** — a descriptive name
- **Type** — expense, income, or transfer
- **Account** — which account the money flows from (or to, for income)
- **Category** — optional label for budgeting
- **Amount** — the transaction amount
- **Frequency** — how often it recurs
- **Start date** — when the schedule begins
- **End date** — optional, for schedules that stop after a certain date
- **Counter-account** — for transfers, the destination account

## Recurrence Options

Shilling supports several recurrence patterns:

- **One-time** — happens once on the start date
- **Weekly** — repeats every week, with a day-of-week selector for choosing which days
- **Biweekly** — repeats every two weeks
- **Monthly by date** — repeats on the same calendar date each month (e.g., the 15th)
- **Monthly by day** — repeats on the same weekday position each month (e.g., the 2nd Friday)
- **Yearly** — repeats once a year on the same date

## Exceptions

You can add per-date exceptions to any schedule:

- **Skip** — suppress the occurrence for a specific date
- **Override** — change the amount or account for a specific date without modifying the schedule itself

Exceptions are managed from the schedule editing form.
